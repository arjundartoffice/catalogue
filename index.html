ABOUT THE COMPANY

Dart Networks Pvt. Ltd. maintains its head office in Rajendra Place, New Delhi, operating as a specialized turnkey system integrator for Extra-Low Voltage (ELV) automation, campus fiber-optic networks, IP CCTV surveillance, and Building Management Systems (BMS).

We bridge civil infrastructure with high-performance IP technology. From design consultation and CAD pathway schematics to Fluke certification testing and lifecycle SLA support, Dart Networks delivers high-availability, zero-downtime environments tailored for IT parks, commercial real estate, data centers, and industrial facilities.
